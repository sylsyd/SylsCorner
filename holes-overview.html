2. Threads that run all unit

Notebook sections set up in L1. They are the revision material for a closed-book test.

Dig 5

Every reading lesson opens with 5 quick recall questions on earlier chapters, answers revealed on screen. Builds the facts for Q1-6, 9, 11 in a closed-book test.

Character Files

One notebook page each for the 7 test characters: Stanley, X-Ray, Zero, Kate Barlow, Mr Pendanski, the Warden, Mr Sir. Format: 2 details + 1 signature + chapter. Mirrors Q7 exactly.

Symbol Tracker

The 8 symbols on the test list (lizards, sunflower seeds, weather, spiced peaches, onions, race, smelly feet, humour), logged with chapter and meaning whenever they appear.

Three Timelines

Latvia (Elya), Green Lake 110 years ago (Kate and Sam), the present (Stanley). Feeds Q12-14, Q16, Q18 and the Q19 summary.

Back It Up frames

"...because...", "We know this because in chapter __...", "This is significant because... It links to...". Used for Q8 and Q12-17.

Friday Fact Check

10-question quiz, self-marked from the screen. Weeks 1-5 (week 5's moves to L35).

5. Teacher notes

Closed book assumed

You weren't sure. The threads work either way; if it turns out to be open book, swap some Dig 5 starters for "find it fast" page hunts.

Reading load

About 1,500-3,300 words per reading lesson (10-22 min of read-aloud or audiobook). Heaviest: L5 (Elya's story) and L34 (ch 46-48). The book finishes on Week 6 Monday, one lesson later than first suggested, so no lesson goes over about 22 min of reading.

Sensitive content

Ch 26: Sam is killed because he is Black, and the novel uses the period term "Negro". Ch 30: the "slave" taunts. Prepare students before reading; Q16 and the Race symbol depend on it.

Mark-scheme traps (from the answer key)

Q2: "he's the leader" scores 0. Q13: singing must be THE family song (0.5 for just "a song"). Q7e Mr Pendanski: kindness 0.5, cruelty 1. Q19: 90-110 words.

Q18 settings

The key only shows Camp Green Lake. The plan covers all three settings in case the student copy lists more.

Film

Holes (2003, PG), about 117 min. The Thursday double gives 110 min.
