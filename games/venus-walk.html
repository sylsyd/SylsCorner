How to use this in class

Setting & atmosphere

Send students in before they read, or after a first read and before the discussion. The job is not to find everything — it is to notice how the place feels, and to collect the details they can point at later.

Task

  1. Collect at least eight observations. Stay outside for the whole hour of sun.
  2. Pick the one detail that made the world feel most closed in. Be ready to say why that one.
  3. After the sun goes, walk back to the schoolroom and look at the closet door again.
