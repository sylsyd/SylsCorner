Same Scene, Four People
Starter EVIDENCE 0/18

How to run it

A ten to fifteen minute starter for the whole class, on the board. You drive; they argue.

The rule of the game

Nobody in these scenes is described. You are never told that someone is nervous or dishonest or kind. You only get what a camera in the corner would catch. Personality is the thing you work out from what people do.

Running a scene

  • Read the four panels aloud, or have four students read one each.
  • Take votes on the trait cards before you assign anything. Make someone justify it with a line.
  • Two of the six trait cards fit nobody. They are the ones students want most.
  • Reveal, then read the verdict lines out. The disagreements are the lesson.

Keys

  • 1–6 pick a trait card, then A–D to place it on a person
  • Enter reveal / next · H this sheet · M sound · R restart
  • Everything also works by clicking. Click a filled slot to take the card back.

After the game

The sentence frame on the reveal screens is the writing task: [Name] is [trait]. We know because [action]. Ask for four of them about someone in the class novel, with no feeling words allowed.
