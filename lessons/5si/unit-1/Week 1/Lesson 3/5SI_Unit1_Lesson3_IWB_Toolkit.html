Working definitions

Explicit

The words directly state it.

Implicit

The text communicates it indirectly.

Inference

The reader’s evidence-based conclusion.

Implicit meaning belongs to the text. An inference is your account of it.

05–15 · Model

Evidence is not
proof by itself.

The reasoning builds the bridge.

Step 1 · Evidence

“They looked at each other
and then looked away.”

Short · exact · relevant

Step 2 · Inference

The children feel ashamed of what they have done.

Plausible conclusion, not plot paraphrase.

Step 3 · Reasoning

Why does looking away suggest shame?

Avoiding one another’s gaze suggests they cannot comfortably face their shared responsibility. Their hesitation contrasts with their earlier excitement.

Spot the fake bridge

Weak

This shows shame because they feel guilty.

Same idea. Different hat.

Useful

Looking away is an act of avoidance after they remember Margot.
