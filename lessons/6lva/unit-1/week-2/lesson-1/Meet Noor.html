Game 3

The adjective chain

Four minutes · standing circle · no writing

  • Say your name and an adjective that starts with the same letter.
  • “I am Maya and I am messy.”
  • The next person says: “She is Maya and she is messy. I am and I am .”
  • Keep the chain going all the way round.
  • If the chain breaks, start again from that person.

Read the text twice. The second time, look for the words that tell you why and when.

1
My name is Noor Haddad. I am eleven years old and I am in Year 7. I am from Manchester, in the north of England, but my family is Lebanese. At home we speak Arabic and English.
2
I live in a small flat near the park with my mother, my father and my little brother, Sami. He is six. He is very noisy! We have got a cat. Her name is Biscuit and she is orange and lazy.
3
I am quite shy at school, but I am not shy at home. I feel nervous when the teacher asks me a question in front of the class, because my English spelling is bad. I am good at drawing and I am good at maths.
4
On Mondays and Wednesdays I play football with my cousins. I don't go swimming because I don't like cold water. Every evening I draw comics in a black notebook. I have got forty-two comics now.
5
My birthday is on the 3rd of March. This year I would like a new bike, and I would like to be more brave.

Unit 1 · Me and Us · Writing

Now write your own introduction

Eight sentences about you. Noor's text is your model. Choose the column that fits you today.

  • Your name, your age, your class
  • Where you are from, and the languages you speak at home
  • Who you live with
  • One thing you have got (use a number)
  • One sentence with because and one sentence with when
  • One thing you are good at, and one thing you would like to change

Unit 1 · Me and Us · Finish

Before you go

Pick one. The first two need the writing you have just done.

Closer 1

Guess who

Five minutes · whole class · use their own writing

  • Papers to the front. No names read out.
  • I read one introduction. You listen for the clues: the numbers, the pets, the hobbies.
  • Hand up when you know. One guess each.
  • If nobody guesses, the writer stands up and reads their best sentence again.
Closer 2

Three-word portrait

Three minutes · pairs · speaking only

  • Look at the person on your right.
  • Say three adjectives about them. All three must be kind.
  • “He is funny, tall and quiet.”
  • Now they say three about you.
  • Stuck? Steal an adjective from Noor's text.
Closer 3

One question for Noor

Three minutes · written · good after the reading

  • Write one question you would ask Noor.
  • It must start with a question word: What, Where, Who, When, Why, How.
  • Not a question the text already answers.
  • Read it out. The class decides: is it a good question?
