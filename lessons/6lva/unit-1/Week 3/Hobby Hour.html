Lesson map (tap to jump)

Plan. Read the four goals aloud, then tap the Français button for the beginners. The times are a guide. If you run late, cut the warm-up to 3 minutes and shorten the sort to 10 items. Keep the last 7 minutes for the wrap-up so the exit ticket happens.

Reading. One silent read (1 minute), then Read aloud while students follow with a finger. The tap tasks recycle the language from stages 2 and 3, and they work for A0 students because they only ask for recognition. The extension asks for evidence, comparison and a change of person, which is enough for the near-native readers.
Writing

Notebook time

Write the date and Hobbies. Do the tasks in order. Write full sentences. Date et titre, puis les exercices dans l'ordre.

1Likes and dislikes

Use the hobby wall. Add -ing if you need it.

  1. 3 sentences: I love / I like ...
  2. 2 sentences: I don't like / I hate ...

I love badminton. I hate running.

2Play, go or do?

Write the full sentences with the correct verb.

  1. I play chess with my grandpa.
  2. She goes running every morning.
  3. My brother does judo.
  4. We go hiking in the holidays.
  5. They play table tennis after school.

3How often?

Rewrite with the right word. The number is days a week.

  1. I play video games. (0)I never play video games.
  2. She goes swimming. (7)She always goes swimming.
  3. We do yoga. (2)We sometimes do yoga.
  4. He plays chess. (5)He usually plays chess.

4About Sam

Write 4 sentences about Sam. Watch the verb: he plays, he goes, he does.

⚽play footballusually
🏊go swimmingnever
🎸play the guitarsometimes
🥋do karatealways
Sam usually plays football. Sam never goes swimming. Sam sometimes plays the guitar. Sam always does karate.

5My partner

Use the answers from the spinner. Write 3 sentences about your partner.

  1. ___ likes / doesn't like ...
  2. ___ (usually) plays / goes / does ... every ...
  3. ___ plays / goes / does ... with ...

Ali likes swimming. He goes swimming with his mum.

6My hobby paragraph

Copy and complete.

My favourite hobby is . I it every . I like it because it is . I don't like because .

Finish each beginning.

  • My favourite hobby is ...
  • I ... (how often) ...
  • I like it because ...
  • I don't like ... because ...

No frames. Write 5 or more sentences.

  • 1 play / go / do phrase
  • 1 how often word
  • 1 because sentence
  • 1 hobby you hate
  • 1 adjective nobody else will use
Extension

A. Acrostic: H O B B I E S

Write the letters down the page. Each line starts with that letter and says something true about your hobbies.

B. But and because

Write 3 sentences: I like swimming but I don't like running because ...

C. My perfect Saturday

Write 5 sentences. Use first, then, after that and 3 how often words.

Writing. Everything is visible at once, so students can work through the tasks in their own order. Tasks 1 to 4 are for everyone. Task 5 reuses the spinner answers, so it is a real communication task. Choose the tier for task 6 from the Week 1 diagnostic: A is gap-fill, B is beginnings, C has no frames. Extension A is the acrostic from Week 5. Tap Model answers to mark tasks 2, 3 and 4.
Fun

Two truths and a lie

Two sentences are true. One is a lie. Which one? Then write your own. Deux vraies phrases, une fausse. Laquelle ?

Tap a sentence to edit it. Tap Guess to vote.
Team points

Exit ticket: I can...

Optional homework: ask someone at home 3 hobby questions and write 3 sentences.

Wrap-up. Edit the three sentences to be about you, then tap the lock buttons to choose the lie (they appear when Teacher notes is on). Students guess as a class or in teams. Then pairs write and swap their own set of three. Finish with the exit ticket: tap a button to cycle through Not yet, Nearly and Yes.
