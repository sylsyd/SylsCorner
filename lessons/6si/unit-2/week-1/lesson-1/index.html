The novel is called Holes.
What can a "hole" be?

Use a frame:

A hole can be a ___ in the ___.
A hole can also mean ___ , for example ___ .
If someone is "in a hole", they are ___ .

Think about: the ground... a sock... a story... a person's life...

• a pit you dig in the ground
• a gap or a missing piece (a hole in a sock, a hole in a story)
• "in a hole" = in trouble, stuck in a bad situation
Keep in mind: the title may mean more than one thing. Watch for it as we read.

WATCH ME Describing a setting = PLACE + TIME

Vague or precise?

Frostmere was a fishing village on the far north coast of Scotland. Its grey stone cottages huddled together against the wind, and in winter the sea froze along the harbour wall. In 1890, nobody there had a car: people still travelled by horse and cart.

Vague (no marks):
"It's a village by the sea. It's in the past."
Precise:
Place: Frostmere, a small fishing village on the far north coast of Scotland, with grey stone cottages and a freezing harbour.
Time: winter, in 1890, when people still used horses and carts.
Precise = ✓ the name   ✓ where exactly   ✓ 2 details you can see or feel   ✓ when (year / era, season)
Spoken. Ask: "What makes the second one better?" Collect the 4 checklist points before revealing them at the bottom. This model is invented on purpose so students don't copy a Holes answer.
