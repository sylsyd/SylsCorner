Check your answers · 4 min

Click each number. Tick it, or fix it in a different colour.

  1. Grandmas Lily's grandma is crinkly and smiley, with white hair, and cooks big meals. Conor's grandma wears trouser suits, dyes her hair, still has a job and doesn't really cook.
  2. Future Grandma wants Conor to come and live with her.
  3. Mum calls When Mum calls, Grandma rushes to her, holds her and rubs her back while she is sick.
  4. Hope Conor hoped the monster had come to help him and "topple" his enemies, like Grandma.
  5. Harsh or caring At first Grandma seems harsh because she says "Less of your cheek". However, she cares because she promises he will have "a home ... With someone who'll love you and care for you."
  6. Hint The monster looks at Grandma's window. This suggests that its story might cause trouble ("havoc") for Grandma, the person Conor sees as his enemy.
